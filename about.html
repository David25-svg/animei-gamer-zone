About AniMéi Gamer Zone

GAMING PEOPLE.
EVENT OPERATORS.

AGZ was built around a simple idea: gaming activations work better when the people running them understand both the event and the audience.

What we do

We support game publishers, developers, brands, agencies, and event organizers with the practical work required to execute gaming activations in the United States.

That can mean staffing a booth, sourcing gaming hardware, supporting printed materials and logistics, coordinating event requirements, engaging players, operating demos, collecting feedback, or simply being the local team that can solve problems when the show is live.

How we work

We aim to operate as an extension of the client team: practical, responsive, player-aware, and focused on execution.
